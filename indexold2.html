About the studio

Independent geospatial work, grounded in real places.

Guided Insight Studio is an independent consultancy based in Hong Kong. I work at the intersection of location, movement, infrastructure, environmental context, and decision-making—combining research, spatial analysis, and clear visual communication.

The current portfolio concentrates on Hong Kong road risk. The same approach extends naturally to urban systems, mobility, environmental monitoring, maritime activity, and other questions where geography changes the answer.
